Zooming in does not add pixels. It makes each pixel cover a smaller piece of the sample. Like stepping closer to a window with the same number of panes: each pane shows less. That is why 1 mm fills more pixels at higher magnification.

How the scale was found

pixels per mm = 7.10 × magnification

Simple picture explanation with a zoom slider →

A mm ruler was photographed at 7 magnifications (14.7x to 175.5x) on 4 Nov 2026. The spacing between 1 mm ticks was measured in pixels on each photo. Pixels per mm is directly proportional to magnification, so the line is fitted through zero (R² = 0.987).

The camera was moved between photos, so single photos sit up to about 15% off the line. The fitted line averages those errors out. Most reliable from 35x to 175x.
